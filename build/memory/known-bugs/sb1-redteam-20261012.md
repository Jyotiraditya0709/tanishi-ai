Full write-up and fixes: `runs/redteam-SB1-20261012.md`. Proofs: `tests/sandbox/test_redteam_sb1.py` (strict xfail).

1. CRITICAL: AR2 `run_python_tests` execs the candidate's code in AR1's unconfined verifier stage.
2. CRITICAL: macOS `kern.procargs2` lets a candidate read argv/env of every same-uid process; no sandbox-exec rule
   gates it. Needs a separate uid, or Docker/VM, to close.
3. HIGH: `network=True` grants AF_UNIX connect to any path (docker.sock, ssh-agent).
4. HIGH: read paths are whole trees; `data/db.sqlite` under the repo root is readable (AR1 passes sys.path).
5. HIGH: worktree copy includes `*.db`, `*.sqlite*`, `.git`.
6. MEDIUM: `chflags uchg` in scratch makes `rollback()` raise `FileExistsError` and leaves files; `close()` leaks scratch.
7. MEDIUM: no process-count limit; no disk quota in scratch.
8. LOW: `_not_copied` is case-sensitive (`Vault/`, `.ENV` copied).

Until 1 and 2 are fixed, nothing may treat the sandbox as holding a key or an exam out of a candidate's reach on macOS.
